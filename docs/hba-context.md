# HBA program — working summary (from 6 source docs, Sep 2026)

**Deliverable:** HBA Presence Page (module 1) built + clickable Showcase of Homes prototype (module 2), demo-able to HBA EOs at NAHB Area 9 Leadership Conference, Chicago, Nov 12–13, 2026. Prototype owner: Hunter (+ Zach/design).

**Positioning:** Hoodle doesn't replace the HBA's site or AMS (dues/events/invoicing, $3.6–10K/yr). It gives the HBA the buyer-facing storefront their platform never did — free, zero-maintenance, pre-populated and claimable. "NAHB fights the political battles; nobody helps builders generate business. Hoodle does." Not a revenue play from HBAs; value flows to HBAs and members.

**Gaps no HBA site fills:** no buyer→builder traffic; Parade attendees vanish (~10K/event); fragmented (parade on separate domain/app/tickets vendor); flat name-and-phone directories; small HBAs invisible; no REALTOR connection; no portable membership proof; join pages sell golf and discounts, not business.

**Presence Page outline**
- P0: 1 Hero (name, logo, territory, value line, member count, Join CTA) · 2 Member directory as rich Hoodle builder pages + associate-member slot; HBA badge on every member page linking back · 3 Parade/Showcase module (dates, hours, tickets, map, contact, sponsors, links to neighborhoods/builders/model homes) · 4 Buyer capture & nurture (save homes/builders, contact; persists after event) · 5 Market map (every builder in territory: members vs not-yet — buyer discovery + HBA recruitment tool) · 6 Join, reframed around leads/visibility/badge first, advocacy second; associate-member angle
- P1: 7 Events & news (light) · 8 Sponsors wall · 9 Advocacy wins (supporting, e.g. "$20 back per $1 dues")
- P2 (leave room): Education/CE · Job board · Awards

**Ron's 7 value props:** Recruit · Retain · Know your market · Event engine · REALTOR bridge · Non-dues revenue · Associate members.

**Showcase of Homes module:** per-event page: map, hours, tickets, participating builders → neighborhood → builder → model-home page (new page type). Gamified buyer registration (proof: Champaign IL, ~6,500 pre-registered, daily sponsor giveaways $250+). Buyer accounts: rate homes loved best/least, private notes, save products/features (sink, tankless heater, movie room). Buyer-builder nurture after event. Sponsors = product companies. Pitch: "here is what your spring showcase becomes."

**Reference data:** Denver = beachhead. Parade of Homes Denver: Aug 6–23, Thu–Sun 12–5, $12 online tickets. Colorado Springs Parade: 72nd year, $18, app with voting + route planning. 8 Colorado local HBAs. Dallas BA = closest consumer-facing model. KC & Greenville = live relationships.

**Chicago CTA:** join waitlist / select market / schedule follow-up / designate HBA point person. Risk: too many features vs a clear narrative.
